# Crown – App Store listing

**Name:** Crown: Hairline Tracker
**Subtitle:** Hair Loss Progress Scanner

**Promotional text:**
Track your hairline with guided monthly scans and private on-device analysis. See real change over time instead of guessing in the mirror.

**Keywords:**
hair loss,hairline,receding,balding,minoxidil,thinning,scalp,tracker,progress,photo,temple,routine

**Description:**
Crown helps you track your hairline with consistent, guided photos and clear numbers, so you can see real change over months instead of guessing in the mirror.

GUIDED, CONSISTENT SCANS
• A face outline and live checks for centering, distance, head angle and light
• Your previous scan appears as a faint guide so every photo lines up
• Front, left temple, right temple and crown angles in about two minutes

ON-DEVICE ANALYSIS
• Hairline position, temple recession and crown coverage estimates
• A Crown score from 0 to 100 and a Norwood-style stage estimate
• Hairline markers drawn on your photo
• A photo quality grade, so lighting changes aren't mistaken for hair changes

SEE YOUR PROGRESS
• Trend chart with journal markers for treatments, haircuts and life events
• Before and after slider, side-by-side compare and animated timelapse
• Insights on how your score and routine adherence are tracking
• A PDF progress report you can share with your doctor or dermatologist

STAY CONSISTENT
• Routine tracker with daily and weekly steps and streaks
• Daily shed counter with a 14-day trend
• Monthly check-in and routine reminders

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Photos and analysis stay on your iPhone and are excluded from iCloud backups
• No ads, no analytics SDKs, no uploads
• Optional Sign in with Apple, plus Face ID app lock
• Delete any scan, all data, or your account at any time

CROWN PRO
Your first scan is free. Crown Pro unlocks unlimited check-ins, full history and trend, compare and timelapse, doctor-ready PDF reports, and routine and shed tracking.
• Weekly: $2.99 per week with a 3-day free trial
• Yearly: $29.99 per year
Prices may vary by region. Payment is charged to your Apple ID at confirmation of purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the end of the period. Manage or cancel in your App Store account settings. Any unused portion of a free trial is forfeited when you purchase a subscription.

IMPORTANT
Crown provides visual estimates for personal progress tracking only. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat or recommend treatment for any condition. Talk to a doctor or dermatologist about hair loss.

**Review notes:**
Crown works without an account. Sign in with Apple is optional and only personalises the app (name on reports). All photos and analysis stay on the device; there is no server.
To test: complete onboarding (tap "Not now" on the sign-in step), then tap the centre scan button. The first scan is free. If the camera is unavailable, use "Import" to choose a clear, front-facing photo with the forehead visible. Crown Pro (weekly with a 3-day trial, or yearly) unlocks additional scans, history, compare, timelapse, PDF reports and routine tracking; Restore Purchases is in the paywall and in the You tab.
Crown is a tracking tool only. It makes no diagnosis or treatment claims and shows a disclaimer in onboarding, results, and settings.
