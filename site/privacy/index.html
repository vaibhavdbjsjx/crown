Privacy Policy

Last updated October 2026

In short

Crown is built so your photos never leave your iPhone. There is no Crown server that receives your data, and the app contains no analytics or advertising SDKs.

Photos and analysis

Progress photos are stored in Crown's private storage on your device and excluded from iCloud backups. Hairline, temple and crown estimates are calculated on your device with Apple's Vision framework. Images and measurements are never uploaded, shared or sold.

Sign-in

If you sign in with Apple or Gmail, Crown receives your name, email address and an account identifier. These are stored only in your device's Keychain and used to personalise the app and your reports. You can sign out or delete your account in the app at any time.

Camera and notifications

Camera access is used only to take progress photos. Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. Crown never sees your payment details.

Children

Crown is not directed at children under 13.
